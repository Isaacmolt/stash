留己看 — 隱私權政策

最後更新:2026 年 8 月 10 日

我們不收集任何資料

「留己看」是一款完全在你裝置上運作的 App。你存入的文章、連結、分類與所有設定,只儲存在你的裝置本機,不會上傳到任何伺服器,開發者與任何第三方都無法存取。

資料儲存

所有資料儲存於裝置上的 App 專屬容器(App Group),僅供主 App、分享擴充功能與桌面小工具之間共用。刪除 App 即會刪除全部資料。

網路連線

當你存入一個連結時,App 會直接向該網頁抓取標題、摘要與縮圖作為快照。此連線由你的裝置直接發出,不經過任何中介伺服器,我們也不會記錄。

通知

每週回顧通知為選擇性功能,經你同意後才啟用,且完全在裝置本機排程。

第三方服務與追蹤

本 App 不包含任何廣告、分析工具或第三方 SDK,不進行任何形式的追蹤。

Read Later — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

We collect no data

Read Later runs entirely on your device. Everything you save — articles, links, categories, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and neither the developer nor any third party can access it.

Storage

All data lives in the app's private container (App Group), shared only between the main app, the share extension, and the widget. Deleting the app deletes all data.

Network

When you save a link, the app fetches the page's title, summary, and thumbnail directly from that website as a snapshot. This request goes straight from your device — no intermediary servers, no logging.

Notifications

The weekly recap notification is optional, enabled only with your permission, and scheduled entirely on-device.

Third parties & tracking

This app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. No tracking of any kind.
